# Dataset Card

EmpatheticDialogues V1 is the project-format conversion of the official
EmpatheticDialogues English test split.

- Records: 2,542 conversations
- Labels: all 32 project emotion labels
- Source: human-authored crowdsourced conversations grounded in emotional situations
- Provenance: `human_authored_emotion_grounding`
- License: CC BY-NC 4.0
- Source archive SHA-256: `56f234d77b7dd1f005fd365bb17769cfe346c3c84295b69bc069c8ccb83be03d`

The expected label is the source conversation-level grounding emotion. It is
not an AI-generated target, but it is also not an independently adjudicated
utterance-level label.
